\subsubsection{Mô tả công việc}

As a QA Automation Intern, you won't just be learning - you'll be building, shipping, and helping shape how we ensure quality at scale. Your work will include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Structured Learning \& Onboarding:} Join the Tyme Internship Program and dive into hands-on training on the TymeX automation ecosystem - covering end-to-end API testing, mobile automation across Android and iOS, and integrating our test frameworks with Slackbot commands for one-click test execution.
    
    \item \textbf{Mentorship-Driven Practice:} Pair with experienced QA engineers who'll guide you in designing, writing, and maintaining end-to-end automated tests that raise product quality and shorten our time to market. Get hands-on with AI-powered testing approaches - including self-healing locators and AI-assisted test generation.
    
    \item \textbf{Real Agile Experience:} Embed with an Agile team and contribute to iteration planning, requirements analysis, and the deployment process - seeing first-hand how modern fintech delivery actually works.
    
    \item \textbf{Lending Domain Deep-Dive:} Build a working understanding of how a lending product operates end-to-end - from instant credit eligibility and installment plan setup to scheduled repayments and collections - and translate that knowledge into meaningful test scenarios.
    
    \item \textbf{From Requirements to Tests:} Read business and functional specs alongside the squad, then design and automate test scenarios that cover both happy paths and the tricky edge cases - the kind that matter most in financial products.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Các kĩ năng yêu cầu}

\textbf{Must-have:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Educational Background:} Must be a fresh graduate or a third/final-year student in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related technical field.
    \item \textbf{Computer Science Fundamentals:} Solid grasp of core concepts including the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), and basic Database Management \& SQL/noSQL.
    \item \textbf{Programming Foundation:} Working knowledge of Java - the core language of our automation stack. Comfortable writing clean, readable code from coursework, personal projects, or internships.
    \item \textbf{Quality \& Testing Mindset:} A natural curiosity about how products work - and an instinct to find the cases that break them. Familiarity with software testing fundamentals (test cases, regression, manual vs. automation) from coursework, projects, or self-learning is a plus.
    \item \textbf{Modern Dev Tooling Awareness:} Basic exposure to Git, REST APIs, and a conceptual understanding of CI/CD pipelines - you don't need to be an expert, just unafraid of them.
    \item \textbf{Self-Motivation:} Passionate about self-improvement, comfortable working independently with minimal supervision, and unafraid to ask questions when stuck.
    \item \textbf{Problem-Solving \& Critical Thinking:} Strong analytical ability - can break down a complex problem, isolate the root cause, and reason carefully through edge cases.
    \item \textbf{Collaboration \& Communication:} Excellent teamwork; thrives in an Agile teams and communicates clearly with both technical and non-technical teammates.
